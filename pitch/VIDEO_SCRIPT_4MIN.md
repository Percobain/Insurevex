## Q&A one-liners

- **"Would that $62 Google policy really have been sold?"** Yes. The engine priced it from data up to 23 Oct, and it was well under the refusal threshold. Alibaba, which was already swinging, was refused.
- **"Isn't this 2008 again?"** No loans, no borrowing, no forced selling. You can only insure stock you own, and every payout is locked upfront.
- **"What's the real-world asset?"** Tokenised Treasury bills behind the savers' guarantee; the savings note as a tokenised bond on Hedera; and real stocks on 23 exchanges, checked through tokenised shares or broker statements.
- **"Isn't retail taking the first loss?"** By choice: it's the insurer's seat, with the risk shown up front. SAVE is for money with a mandate not to lose.
