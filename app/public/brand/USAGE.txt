Use SVG for web, slides and print at any size; use PNG where SVG is unsupported.
All SVG artwork is traced vector paths, with no embedded bitmap or font dependency.
Use the dark artwork on light backgrounds and the light artwork on dark backgrounds.
Preserve aspect ratio. Do not retype the lettering, add effects or fill its cutouts.
Allow at least one symbol-height of clear space around large standalone placements.
